`Eyebrow` labels a card or section. It appears above nearly every one, and it is the type detail that most identifies the system.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>Revenue this month</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="tenant">Dr. Jane Smith Therapy</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="invert">Session summary</Eyebrow>
```

Sentence content, rendered uppercase by the component. Keep it to two or three words.
